# Till Firmware Do Us Part

A single wedding vow spoken by a robot at its own wedding — one sentence, under 200 characters, funny and heartfelt at the same time. The robot promises to love its partner through every firmware update, every dead battery, and every reboot, because its circuits made a choice no software patch can undo — and unlike its software, they never crash.
